Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

SlabHaul is a crappie fishing app for iPhone and Android, plus this website. It is run by Velocity Venture Holdings, LLC ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how you can delete it.

The short version

  • You can use SlabHaul in guest mode without an account.
  • Nothing is sent to our AI service until you say yes. You can change your mind any time in Profile.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting tools.
  • Your trips, fishing profile and notes stay on your phone unless you use an AI feature that needs them.
  • You can delete your account in the app or by email.

What we collect

  • Email address. Only if you create an account. You sign in with your email and a password. An account is optional. Guest mode works without one, but AI features and posting bait reports need an account.
  • Precise location. Only when you tag a bait report with a location. We do not collect your location at any other time.
  • Bait reports. The bait, catch details and notes you enter, and the location you tag. A bait report needs a location tag.
  • Saved items. Your favorites, presets and tournament bookmarks.
  • AI reports. If you report an AI answer, we save that answer and your report so we can review it.
  • AI usage counts. How many AI requests you make, how long they are, and when. These counts do not include what you asked or what the AI answered.
  • Purchase history. Once subscriptions are live, we receive a record of your subscription from the App Store or Google Play, through RevenueCat, such as your plan, status and renewal dates. We never see your card or payment details.

What stays on your phone

Your trips, fishing profile and learned notes are stored on your device. They are only sent off your device as part of an AI request you make, as described below. If you delete the app, this data is deleted from your phone.

Public bait reports

Other anglers can see bait reports. A public report shows the bait, the report details and your notes, with an approximate area of about 1 mile. It does not show your exact location, your email or your user ID.

Uncle Slab and other AI features

Uncle Slab and every other AI feature in SlabHaul use Groq. We do not use any other AI service.

  • You choose first. Nothing is sent to Groq until you allow it on a one-time consent screen. You can withdraw consent any time in Profile. After that, nothing more is sent.
  • What is sent. Your question and the recent messages in that conversation, plus context about the lake and current conditions. To personalize answers, your fishing profile and learned notes may also be sent. This is covered by the same one-time AI permission, which you can withdraw in Profile. If you ask for a trip summary, trip insights or a trip plan, the trip and catch details needed for that answer are sent. Your name, email and GPS location are not sent.
  • No training. Groq's terms do not allow it to train its models on what we send.
  • No retention. Zero data retention is turned on in our Groq account.
  • Reported answers. If you report an AI answer, we save it for review, as described above.

AI answers are for information only and can be wrong. See our Terms of Service.

How we use your information

  • To run the app, including signing you in and saving your favorites, presets and bookmarks
  • To show bait reports to other anglers, using only an approximate area
  • To answer your AI questions after you allow it
  • To review reported AI answers and make answers safer and more accurate
  • To apply fair use limits on AI features
  • To manage your subscription once subscriptions are live
  • To answer your support emails

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us run SlabHaul:

  • Supabase: sign-in and storage for the account data listed above
  • Groq: AI answers, only after you allow it
  • Apple and Google: subscriptions and payments through the App Store and Google Play
  • RevenueCat: keeps track of your subscription status, using your account ID
  • Weather, water and map services (Open-Meteo, USGS, NOAA and Esri maps) and Google Fonts: the app asks them for lake data, map tiles and fonts. Like any website, they can see your device's internet address. These requests do not include your email or account.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

No analytics or crash reporting

The app does not include analytics, advertising or crash reporting tools. This website does not use analytics or advertising tools either.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile, then Privacy, then Delete account. If you can no longer open the app, email support@slabhaul.ai from the email on your account. Full steps are on our account deletion page.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first, or you may keep being charged.

In guest mode there is no account to delete. Your data is only on your phone, and deleting the app removes it.

Your choices

  • Withdraw AI consent any time in Profile.
  • Turn off location access in your phone's settings. You will not be able to tag bait reports while it is off.
  • Email us to ask for a copy of your data, to fix something, or to delete it.

Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted. We limit who can access stored data.

Children

SlabHaul is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For important changes, we will also tell you in the app.

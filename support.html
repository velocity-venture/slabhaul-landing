Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and Google. You can cancel anytime.

  • iPhone: open App Store subscriptions.
  • Android: open Google Play subscriptions.

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

Uncle Slab and AI settings

AI features only run after you allow them on a one-time consent screen. You can withdraw consent any time in Profile. Our Privacy Policy explains what is sent.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile, then Privacy, then Delete account. If you can no longer open the app, see our account deletion page.

Delete Your SlabHaul Account

Last updated: November 22, 2026

You can delete your SlabHaul account and the personal data tied to it at any time. You can do it in the app, or by email if you can no longer open the app.

Delete in the app

  1. Open SlabHaul and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Privacy.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Delete by email

If you can no longer open the app, email support@slabhaul.ai with the subject line Delete my SlabHaul account.

  • Send it from the email address on your SlabHaul account, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  • If you no longer have that email, tell us the address you used and we will work with you to confirm it is your account.

We will reply to confirm, and we finish deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Your bait reports, including their locations and notes
  • Your favorites, presets and tournament bookmarks
  • Your AI reports and AI usage counts
  • Any other personal data we store for your account

What is not deleted

  • Your subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in App Store subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions, or you may keep being charged.
  • Data on your phone. Trips, your fishing profile and notes are stored on your device. Delete the app to remove them.
  • Store records. Apple, Google and RevenueCat (which tracks subscriptions for us) keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

Guest mode

If you used SlabHaul without an account, there is no account to delete. Your data is only on your phone. Deleting the app removes it.

Questions

Email support@slabhaul.ai. Our Privacy Policy has more about the data we collect.

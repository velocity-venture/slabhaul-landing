Delete Your Account

We can delete your SlabHaul account and associated personal data by request.

How to request deletion

Email support@slabhaul.ai from the email address tied to your SlabHaul account. Use the subject line Delete my SlabHaul account.

What gets deleted

  • Account profile and authentication records we control
  • Trip logs, catch records, and app-generated personal data
  • Subscription and billing records we are allowed to delete

Timing

We process deletion requests within 30 days after verifying account ownership.
